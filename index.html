Calibration

Uses the community firing rule: each charge adds 5 km of range, and elevation scales linearly up to 60°. That works out to elevation = km × 12 ÷ charges. The game’s developer has confirmed the ballistics are this simplified, and players report every shell type uses the same elevation. If a game update changes the ballistics, adjust these two numbers to match the in-game calculator. The firing log and these settings are saved in this browser only.
